Simple Definition

---

## Section 2

Beginner Explanation

---

## Section 3

Real Match Example

---

## Section 4

Visual Diagram

---

## Section 5

Advanced Breakdown

---

## Section 6

Quiz

---

# Writing Standards

Avoid:

* Unexplained jargon
* Long paragraphs
* Assumed knowledge

Prefer:

* Short explanations
* Real examples
* Visual analogies

---

# AI Tutor Content Policy

The AI Tutor should:

1. Search LearnFootball content first.
2. Use platform definitions.
3. Cite lessons where relevant.
4. Avoid contradicting official lessons.
