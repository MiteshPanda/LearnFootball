# TEAM_DATA_PLAN.md

## Purpose

Define the structure, content requirements, and user experience for team profiles in LearnFootball.

A team page should help users understand:

* Who the team is
* Why the team matters
* How the team plays
* Who its legendary players are
* What it has achieved

The page should be educational, visual, and beginner-friendly.
