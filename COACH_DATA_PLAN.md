# COACH_DATA_PLAN.md

## Purpose

Define the structure, content requirements, and user experience for coach profiles in LearnFootball.

Coach pages should help users understand:

* Who the coach is
* What football philosophy they believe in
* Which formations they use
* What tactical innovations they introduced
* Why they are important to football history

Coach pages are educational resources, not merely biographies.

---

# User Goals

A visitor should be able to answer:

* Who is this coach?
* What style of football do they prefer?
* Which teams did they manage?
* What trophies did they win?
* How did they influence football?

within a few minutes.
