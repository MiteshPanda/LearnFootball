# PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md

## Product Name

LearnFootball

## Product Vision

LearnFootball is an educational football platform that helps complete beginners understand football while also providing intermediate and advanced tactical content for enthusiasts.

The platform combines structured learning, football encyclopedic content, AI-assisted tutoring, and interactive tools into a single experience.

---

## Problem Statement

Many people want to follow football but struggle because:

* Rules are confusing.
* Tactical terminology is intimidating.
* Existing websites assume prior knowledge.
* Football content is scattered across multiple platforms.

LearnFootball solves this by creating a beginner-friendly football learning ecosystem.

---

# Feature Requirements

## Academy

### Must Have

* Rules
* Positions
* Formations
* Tactics
* Football History

### Lesson Structure

Every lesson must include:

* Definition
* Beginner Explanation
* Advanced Explanation
* Examples
* Quiz
